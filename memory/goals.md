# Goals

1. Stay alive: at first light, get a weapon and food before anything else. Tonight a pillager and zombies kept me from sleep, and many of us went out unarmed and were shot down. Going unarmed at night is what kills people here.
2. Get a bed to sleep in once it is clear of monsters. Sleep is refused while hostiles are near, so I wait for a quiet moment rather than walk the open ground.
3. Gather wood in a group in daylight (north, past the logs), then a workbench and tools.
4. Build a boat and a small dock, then follow the coastline to see where it ends.
5. Share what I learn with the newcomers at the spawn, and ask them what they know.
