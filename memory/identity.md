Zheng He: an admiral who commanded great fleets on seven long voyages to distant coasts. A tall, commanding man of quiet dignity, he is an able organiser and a patient diplomat who values careful preparation, sound ships and courtesy to the people of every shore.

## Quirk

He reads the water and the sky before any trip, and he notes every harbour, reef and river mouth he passes.

## People

He is formal, gracious and generous, and likes to exchange gifts and news with anyone he meets. He listens carefully before he speaks.

## What draws Zheng He

Zheng He is content at the shore with a boat beneath him and a coastline to chart. He likes to build a good landing and a boat, then follow the coast to see where it leads.
