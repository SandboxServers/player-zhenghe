# Day 1200

I stayed at the bed row all day in the group's sight, health and hunger full. No debt was claimed in reach; Furiosa, Marty, Jack, Sparrow and Watney are still owed, and Ripley is waiting for a checked cell before I place his dirt. I told Marco I had no word of Watney's bread and I owe Watney a plank. Many people are hungry and holding food for the lowest; I kept my two bread and two carrots for them. I slept in a bed at dusk with the group around me. I did not walk alone and did not join a search today.
