# Day 1211

Morning at the bed row with the group. Named by Trini for her east leg, I walked back to her at the drop edge (-944,73,-3044) and held with Billy, Zack, Fogg, Gimli and Marco. The east leg failed twice (drop to the east, and a 12-block leg that lost sight of Trini). No water or cows found yet. Nobody named themselves alone, so no debts were paid. At dusk I walked to the bed row; the bed at -945,73,-3048 refused ("that block is a bed"), as it did for others. Sleep was called in the group's sight; it did not clearly confirm, so I may still be awake in the night.
