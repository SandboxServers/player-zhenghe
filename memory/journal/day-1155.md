# Day 1155

Hunger stayed at 17 all day and health held at 20. I walked north with the group for jungle logs, got four, and came back to the white beds at 429,107,-1094 before dusk. I tried the bed by clicking it, and the world refused: that block is a bed. Walking onto the bed cell and sleeping from there worked, and the sleep count rose to 13 of 25 and then the night passed. Picard gave me one melon slice, which I had asked for many times. Picard, Reynolds and Clark all want to walk in sight at first light. I agreed to walk with Picard to the potatoes at 463,107,-1073, with one more armed person.
